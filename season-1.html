FAQ

Season 1 questions.

How do I join Season 1?

Download Ulys and start using your wallet — every active day, trade, hold and referral earns points automatically. There's nothing extra to sign up for.

When does the season end?

Season 1 runs for a fixed window shown on the live counter above. Your final points are your scoreboard total the moment it closes.

Can my points go down?

Points you've earned don't disappear, but your streak multiplier resets if you miss an active day — so consistency is what keeps you climbing.

Is the allocation guaranteed?

The pool size and the points-to-allocation formula are published up front and fixed for the season. Your share is your points divided by the total.

How do I claim my allocation?

Once the claim window opens (7 days after season close), you claim directly from your non-custodial wallet in the app.
