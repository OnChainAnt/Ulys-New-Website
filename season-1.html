FAQ

Season 1 questions.

How do I join Season 1?

Download Ulys and start using your wallet — every active day, trade, hold and referral earns points automatically. There's nothing extra to sign up for.

When does the season end?

Season 1 runs for a fixed window shown on the live counter above. Your final points are your leaderboard total the moment it closes.

Can my points go down?

Points you've earned don't disappear, but your streak multiplier resets if you miss an active day — so consistency is what keeps you climbing.

How is my rank calculated?

Your rank is simply your total points versus everyone else, updated live. Climb by earning more and keeping your streak alive.
