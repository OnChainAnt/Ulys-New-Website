FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How does idea-based trading work?

Tell Ulys an idea in plain words, like "robots are taking over factories". Ulys builds a basket of tokens and tokenized stocks around it, explains every pick, and lets you adjust the weights before you buy the whole basket in one tap.

What can I put in a basket?

Any token and tokenized stocks, across chains like Ethereum, Solana and Base. Ulys handles the routing, so you never have to bridge or switch chains yourself.

How do I share an idea?

Every basket has its own link. Send it to anyone and they can open it, see every pick and weight, and copy the idea into their own wallet in one tap.

Can I connect other wallets to Ulys?

Ulys is built as your primary smart wallet. Broader connectivity is on the roadmap as the platform expands.

Is the Ulys wallet secure?

Ulys is non-custodial, so you hold your own keys — without the burden of storing a full seed phrase. Your assets are never in someone else's custody.

Is Ulys available outside of the US?

Availability varies by region and is expanding. Download the app to check eligibility where you are.

Which devices does Ulys support?

Ulys is available on iOS via the Apple App Store, with more platforms on the way.
